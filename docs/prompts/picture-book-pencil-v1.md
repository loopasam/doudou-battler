# Picture-book pencil — style candidate v1

This is an exploratory deck style, not yet the locked production direction.

## Visual language

- Contemporary children's picture-book illustration.
- Soft watercolor and gouache with delicate colored-pencil and graphite lines
  on warm, lightly textured paper.
- Visible pigment blooms, slightly imperfect brush edges, simple expressive
  shapes, and gentle daylight.
- A restrained palette built from paper cream, character-specific colors,
  muted sage, pale sky blue, and small warm-gold accents.
- No photorealism, cinematic concept-art lighting, glossy digital finish,
  plastic 3D rendering, or highly detailed fur.

## Card composition

- Compose directly for the game's 318:448 portrait card ratio.
- Use a head-led close portrait. The face is centered, immediately readable,
  and occupies roughly the upper half of the image.
- Preserve generous safe margins around ears, horns, hair, and other defining
  silhouette details.
- Show only enough body to establish the character. Keep backgrounds simple,
  softly painted, and secondary to the face.
- One character only; no text, logos, watermarks, weapons, or clutter.

Every future card using this candidate style should preserve the same medium,
line quality, paper texture, compositional hierarchy, and background restraint.
