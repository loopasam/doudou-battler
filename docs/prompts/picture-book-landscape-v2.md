# Picture-book landscape — production candidate v2

This version keeps the established watercolor, gouache, colored-pencil, and
graphite deck style while composing directly for the artwork window that is
actually rendered inside each game card.

## Canonical format

- Artwork aspect ratio: `268:162` (`1.654:1` landscape).
- Recommended working size: `1072 × 648` or a larger image at the same ratio.
- The complete game card remains `318 × 448`; the artwork is only its upper
  illustration panel.
- Fill the frame edge-to-edge. Do not add letterboxing, a card border, labels,
  numbers, or other UI to the generated image.

## Composition

- Use a close head-and-shoulders portrait designed horizontally from the start.
- Keep the face immediately readable at small size.
- Keep eyes, muzzle or beak, ears, horns, crests, and other critical identity
  features within the inner 80% safe zone.
- Use the side areas for character-defining shapes such as a mane, tail fan, or
  quiet storybook background—not for extra characters or visual clutter.
- Preserve warm paper texture, restrained backgrounds, and the same medium and
  line quality across the complete deck.
