# Cozy Storybook Contenders — style lock v1

Use the uploaded toy photo as the **subject reference only**. Reimagine its defining
colors, materials, silhouette, and personality as a newly illustrated, original
character. Never clean up or publish the photograph itself.

## Collection direction

- Portrait game-card artwork in a tactile, painterly storybook style.
- A cozy handmade fantasy world: blanket forts, fabric hills, cardboard castles,
  softly glowing lanterns, and playful household materials.
- Warm cinematic light, handcrafted depth, visible plush texture, and a clear,
  friendly silhouette.
- Cozy, brave, playful, and suitable for young children; never threatening.
- A warm cream and deep teal environment with coral and amber accents. Preserve
  the subject's own signature colors.
- One full character, centered in a 2:3 portrait with generous card-safe margins.
- No humans, extra characters, text, lettering, logos, watermarks, weapons,
  franchise styling, plastic 3D rendering, or photographic room details.

## Per-character prompt

Add the toy's defining traits, a child-friendly working name and personality, a
simple hero pose, and one small environmental story beat. The first result enters
the private review queue; curation notes drive later variants.
